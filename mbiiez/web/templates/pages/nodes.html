Sync CADED data

Copy from one node into another. Existing accounts keep their PIN and balance; conflicting identities are skipped. Stats keep the highest counter values, rather than adding duplicate snapshots. Ban sync adds bans and keeps local notes; removals and admin privileges stay local.

Imported bans can disconnect banned players immediately. Live stats imports require the new CADED transaction build. Sync does not restart game servers. Backups are kept on the destination node.
