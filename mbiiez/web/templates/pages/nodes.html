Enable Sync CADED data on a remote node to link it to Local in both directions. Accounts, PIN changes, credit earnings and spending, bans and unbans, kills and playtime are shared. Local supplies the initial values for matching identities; remote-only records are preserved and backed up. New transactions and stats events are counted once. If the authority cannot be reached, spending pauses and earnings/stats remain queued. Imported bans may disconnect banned players. No game restart is performed by this checkbox. Unlinking requires a planned stop of the remote node's CADED instances so pending purchases can settle safely.

Sync CADED data

For unlinked nodes only. Imports are blocked while shared data is enabled. Copy from one node into another. Existing accounts keep their PIN and balance; conflicting identities are skipped. Stats keep the highest counter values, rather than adding duplicate snapshots. Ban sync adds bans and keeps local notes; removals and admin privileges stay local.

Imported bans can disconnect banned players immediately. Live stats imports require the new CADED transaction build. Sync does not restart game servers. Backups are kept on the destination node.
