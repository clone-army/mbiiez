Nickname Bans

The game can't ban a name itself, so MBIIEZ checks every player's name when they connect or change it, and kicks them if it matches. Anyone already playing under the name is kicked when you add the ban. Colour codes and capital letters are ignored. Names are easy to change, so for repeat offenders ban their IP or GUID as well, or tick "Also ban their IP" below.
